Income Tax Ordinance, 2001 — First Schedule, Part I, Division I
Rates of tax for individuals whose income under the head "Salary" exceeds
seventy-five per cent of taxable income, as they stood under the Finance Act,
2025 (Tax Year 2026, 1 July 2025 – 30 June 2026).

Encoding source: "Existing rate of tax" column of KPMG Taseer Hadi & Co.,
"A Brief of Finance Act, 2026" (July 2026), cross-checked against published
worked examples (see provenance). The FBR consolidated Ordinance (amended up
to 20.02.2026) is the primary artifact to hash; see TARGET_DOSSIER.md.

S. No. | Taxable income | Rate of tax
1 | Where taxable income does not exceed Rs. 600,000 | 0%
2 | Where taxable income exceeds Rs. 600,000 but does not exceed Rs. 1,200,000 | 1% of the amount exceeding Rs. 600,000
3 | Where taxable income exceeds Rs. 1,200,000 but does not exceed Rs. 2,200,000 | Rs. 6,000 plus 11% of the amount exceeding Rs. 1,200,000
4 | Where taxable income exceeds Rs. 2,200,000 but does not exceed Rs. 3,200,000 | Rs. 116,000 plus 23% of the amount exceeding Rs. 2,200,000
5 | Where taxable income exceeds Rs. 3,200,000 but does not exceed Rs. 4,100,000 | Rs. 346,000 plus 30% of the amount exceeding Rs. 3,200,000
6 | Where taxable income exceeds Rs. 4,100,000 | Rs. 616,000 plus 35% of the amount exceeding Rs. 4,100,000

Section 4AB surcharge (salaried persons): 9% of the income tax where taxable
income exceeds Rs. 10,000,000 (rate reduced from 10% to 9% for salaried
persons by the Finance Act, 2025).
